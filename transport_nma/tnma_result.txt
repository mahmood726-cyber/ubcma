VERDICT (honest): the registry-lambda gives the correct RELATIVE selection-severity PATTERN
  (which classes are selection-prone) -- an ORACLE-calibrated correction (kappa=B) recovers
  truth better than the unadjusted NMA under real selection (B>0) and is ~inert at B=0. But
  a FIXED, uncalibrated kappa=0.5 OVER-CORRECTS and HARMS everywhere. So registry pub-bias
  adjustment in NMA needs (a) a data-driven strength (funnel asymmetry, not a fixed kappa)
  and (b) a selection-presence gate -- exactly the univariate-AdaptShrink discipline. The
  registry lambda supplies the direction/ranking of selection severity, not its magnitude.
